# VOON IQ (voon.fi)

Before any work in this repository – copy, UI, images, newsletters, product texts or code that renders
user-facing content – read and apply the `voon-iq-filosofia` skill
(`.claude/skills/voon-iq-filosofia/SKILL.md`). Every text, image and product decision must pass its
philosophy test: who is made visible (the person, not the technology), which act of the story it shows
(sumu, sana, valo), and what the person can do next. Also apply its Nordic work-culture guide
(`.claude/skills/voon-iq-filosofia/nordic.md`): trust, understatement, evidence over claims, promises kept.
